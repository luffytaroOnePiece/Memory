Frequently Asked Questions

Does it last all day long?

If sprayed at the right pulse points, given its highly concentrated EDP formula, it is engineered to last significantly longer than standard EDT perfumes, generally performing well for 6-12 hours depending on skin chemistry.

Is it suitable for specific seasons?

Beardo Godfather is meticulously blended with both fresh top notes (Mint/Lemon) and warm base notes (Musk/Vetiver), making it exceptionally versatile for all seasons—though it excels in cooler evening settings.

Are there any alternatives within Beardo?

For those seeking deeper woods and tobacco, Whisky Smoke is a strong alternative. For an even more premium experience, the Beardo Alpha Extrait De Parfum line offers higher concentrations.
